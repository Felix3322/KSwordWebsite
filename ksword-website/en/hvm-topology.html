Features with additional checks

The diagram uses two logical processors and example VM-exit counts; these are not readings from your machine. AMD SVM/NPT remains experimental.

Host

With VBS enabled, Hyper-V already owns VMX root. This example runs KSword inside a child VM with nested virtualization enabled.

Guest

After residency starts, the existing Windows runs at L2. Its kernel and processes remain the same.

KSword

KSword handles the target’s VM exits at L1. EPT access monitoring, split views and R-1 operations depend on this layer.
