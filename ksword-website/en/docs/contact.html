What to include in a report

  • The downloaded package, release/tag or full patch identifier; state whether it is the Qt6 edition, Light or CLI.
  • The Windows OS build. For HVM/DMA issues, include the CPU, virtual machine or outer hypervisor.
  • The page you opened and the first failing step, with expected and actual results.
  • Original error codes, logs and screenshots. For R0 issues, include Loaded, Protocol, DynData and Capability states.
  • Whether it affects only this target or also other processes and files. Give the approximate frequency if it is intermittent.
  • Redact accounts, keys, tokens and sensitive paths from logs. Keep the complete error and page state visible in screenshots where possible.
